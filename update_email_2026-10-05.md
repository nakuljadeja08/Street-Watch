Street Watch now tracks jobs posted by staffing agencies, alongside the firms'
own career sites.

WHY AGENCIES

Many large employers hand part of their hiring to outside firms. Morgan Stanley
and Barclays use AMS to run parts of their recruiting, and JPMorgan fills its
contract roles through staffing agencies. Those contract and temp-to-hire roles
(operations, KYC, risk, reporting, FP&A) are usually posted only on the
agency's own website, often without naming the bank or company. Until now
Street Watch couldn't see them.

AGENCIES ADDED (4)

  - Robert Half
  - Randstad
  - Michael Page
  - Aston Carter (the finance arm of Allegis, the largest US staffing group)

Street Watch pulls their analyst and associate roles in New York, Chicago and
the Bay Area, in finance fields only (finance, operations, risk, compliance,
treasury, investments). Accounting, payroll, IT, senior and suburban roles are
left out. Each title says whether it's a Contract, Contract-to-hire or Direct
hire role. To see only these, pick "Staffing Agency" under Firm type on the
dashboard.

About 25 agency roles are on the dashboard now. Two tips for agency roles:

  - Applying puts you in touch with the agency's recruiter, who can tell you
    which company the role is at and put you forward for similar roles.
  - Real agencies are paid by the employer. If anyone asks you to pay a fee
    for training, placement or a "guaranteed" job, it isn't a real agency.

ON INTERVIEW TIMING

Companies are now taking more than a month to review applications, which is
why interview invitations are arriving a month or so after you apply. If you
haven't heard back within a few weeks, the application is most likely still in
the queue rather than turned down, so it's worth continuing to apply in the
meantime.
