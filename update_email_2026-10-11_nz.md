Subject: Street Watch update: temporary roles in New Zealand are on your board

Hi Ms Tian,

Since you can also work in New Zealand, I've added it to Street Watch, set to
show temporary roles only. Here's what's new.

HOW TO SEE THEM

There's a new "NZ" button in the location filter, next to NY + JC, SF / Bay
and Chicago. It covers the whole country, though most of the roles are in
Auckland and Wellington. Your morning newsletter will also have a New Zealand
section whenever there are new NZ roles.

WHAT COUNTS AS TEMPORARY

Fixed term or maximum term roles, contract and contract to hire roles,
parental leave cover, secondments and casual roles. The board reads this from
the job title or the job description, and NZ roles carry a "Temporary" or
"Contract" tag so you can see why they're there. Permanent roles are left
out, and so are postings that don't say either way.

THE FIRMS

Most of the firms already on your board have no open roles in New Zealand
right now, so I looked for NZ firms in the same areas you're targeting:

  - Banks: ANZ, BNZ, Heartland Bank
  - Investment banking: Jarden (the biggest NZ investment bank)
  - Investing: NZ Super Fund (the government's sovereign wealth fund), Craigs
    Investment Partners
  - Consulting: BCG Auckland, plus the deals, strategy and economics teams at
    Deloitte and KPMG
  - Fintech: Xero, Airwallex, Sharesies (product and go to market roles, like
    the US fintech firms)
  - Robert Half's New Zealand site, where many temporary finance roles are
    placed

Goldman Sachs, Macquarie and the other global firms already on Street Watch
will show up under NZ automatically whenever they post a temporary role there.

WHAT'S OPEN TODAY

Two roles match right now:

  - BNZ: Technical Business Analyst, a fixed term role in Auckland
  - Robert Half: FP&A Analyst, a 12 month contract in Christchurch

Most NZ openings at these firms are permanent, so expect a handful of new
roles a week rather than a long list. The same experience rules apply as in
the US, with two NZ additions: graduate roles count, and a "Senior Associate"
or "Senior Analyst" role stays on the board if the posting asks for 3 years
of experience or less.

WHERE ELSE TO LOOK

A lot of temporary work in New Zealand goes through recruiting agencies and
Seek (New Zealand's main job site), which Street Watch can't search
automatically. Worth setting up:

  - A saved search on seek.co.nz for "analyst" with the work type set to
    "Contract/Temp", in Auckland and Wellington
  - Registering with the finance teams at Hays, Michael Page and Robert
    Walters in Auckland or Wellington, which fill many contract roles
  - Checking Westpac NZ, ASB and Kiwibank's careers pages for fixed term
    roles, since they can't be added to the board

RECRUITER EMAILS

The "Draft email" button on the Recruiters page now mentions that you're open
to temporary or contract roles in New Zealand (Auckland or Wellington) as
well as roles in New York, Chicago and San Francisco.
