TRACKING A JOB YOU FOUND SOMEWHERE ELSE

Before today, a role that wasn't on the board needed two separate forms: one
to write its cover letter, and another to add it to your tracker, where you
had to type the company, role and link all over again. Now it's one form.

  1. Open the Board view and click "＋ Add your own".
  2. Fill in the firm, role, link and stage (it starts on Applied).
  3. Click "✍ Add role & write cover letter". The role goes on your tracker
     and the letter is written right away, saved with that role.

A few details:

  - Pasting the job description is optional. If you leave it blank, Street
    Watch reads the posting from the link. If it can't, it asks you to paste
    it.
  - "Add role only" tracks the role without writing a letter. Pressing Enter
    in the form does the same, so you won't start a letter by accident.
  - The link box now only accepts a web address, so a job description can't
    end up there by mistake.
  - To reopen a letter later, click "✍ Cover letter" on the role's board
    card (or "✍ Draft" in the list view).
  - If you remove a role from your tracker, its cover letter is deleted with
    it.
  - "Cover letter for an outside job" in the AI panel now opens this same
    form.
