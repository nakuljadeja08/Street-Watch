Hi Ms Tian,

One more addition today: Street Watch now has a Recruiters page, so you can
reach out to recruiters directly instead of only applying to listings.

WHAT'S ON IT

Open Recruiters (next to List, Board and Trends). It lists about 530
recruiters at 143 New York search firms that cover finance roles. The ones
most worth contacting come first: recruiters at "contingency" firms (they're
paid when they place someone, so they want to hear from candidates) and people
with recruiter titles rather than senior partners.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

  - Track your outreach. On any recruiter, log where things stand (Emailed,
    Replied, Call set, Follow up, Not a fit), set a follow-up date and keep
    notes. Marking someone as Emailed sets a reminder for a week later.
  - Draft an email. "Draft email" writes a short, personal first email from
    your resume and that recruiter's focus. You edit it and send it yourself;
    nothing is ever sent for you. (It uses the resume saved under the AI
    button, the same one the cover letters use.)
  - Find recruiters for a role. Each role on the board now has a 👥 button
    showing the recruiters who cover that kind of job, so you can reach out
    about a specific opening.
  - Get reminders. Starting Monday, October 12, the morning email will
    suggest three recruiters to contact each week, and any day it will list
    the follow-ups that are due.

A TIP

A few recruiters a week, each with a short and specific note, works much
better than a big batch. Start with the contingency firms at the top of the
list.

The directory is private: only you and I can see it after signing in.
